# Departments

A **department** is a group of people at CodaCode Game Dev Studios who work on the same kind of job. Someone can belong to more than one department, and each department has **leaders** (who guide the work) and **members** (everyone else who works in it).

Departments are different from **roles**. A role is a person's job title, like Lead Writer. A department is the team they work in, like Writing. See the [Org Chart](../org-chart.md) for how they fit together, or the [Directory](../directory.md) to search everyone.
